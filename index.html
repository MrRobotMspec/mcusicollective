Client Service Policy

A clear framework for working together.

Our goal is to make every project clear, efficient and enjoyable. Please review our working policy before and during your engagement with Mcusi Collective.

Payment & Onboarding

Projects under R1500 require full payment upfront. Projects over R1500 require a 50% deposit to secure the booking and commence work. The remaining balance is payable as agreed. Payments must be made using the official banking details provided by Mcusi Collective.

No eWallet, Cash Send or Money Voucher payments.

Brief & Communication

Work begins once payment and the required project information/content have been received. Clients are expected to provide clear, consolidated feedback to keep projects moving efficiently.

Delays in feedback or content may affect delivery timelines.

Revisions & Changes

Each project includes 3 rounds of revisions. Additional revision rounds are charged at R150 per round.

Changes that significantly alter the original brief, concept or scope may be quoted as additional work.

Timelines & Urgent Work

We aim to respond to feedback and requests promptly. Delivery timelines depend on project scope, workload and client responsiveness.

Urgent or same-day requests are subject to availability and may incur an additional fee.

Cancellation & Approval

Deposits are non-refundable once work has commenced. Completed work may be billed if a project is cancelled.

Clients are responsible for reviewing and approving final artwork. Changes requested after final approval may be treated as additional work.
